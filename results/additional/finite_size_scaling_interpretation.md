# Additional finite-size scaling interpretation

Additional, not part of the official required results: Exact sparse diagonalization on the open TFIM at N=4,6,8,10,12 and 36 fields from g=0.25 to 2.00 in 0.05 steps shows the finite-size trends in N(E1−E0), half-chain entropy, and <Mz^2>/N^2; adjacent-size N-gap crossings from linear interpolation are N=4 vs 6: g≈0.9543, N=6 vs 8: g≈0.9752, N=8 vs 10: g≈0.9843, N=10 vs 12: g≈0.9891. The known infinite-chain critical value is g_c=1. The crossings and changing entropy/order diagnostics near g=1 are consistent with finite-size critical scaling, but this N≤12 open-chain data set does not establish a new critical point, critical exponent, thermodynamic limit, or hardware behavior. N=4 and N=6 sparse E0/E1 values were each checked against all seven official reference files at absolute tolerance 1e-09 (14 size-field pairs; 28 eigenvalue comparisons passed).
