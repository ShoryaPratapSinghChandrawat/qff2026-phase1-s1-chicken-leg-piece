## Optional N=6 bonus: official text, current coverage, and remaining scope

The DOCX gives the bonus as **“Bonuses (up to 5 points, total capped at 100): PS-S1 N = 6 tier”** (P108). Table 11 defines PS-S1 sizes as **“N = 4 (required); N = 6 (optional tier, bonus)”**, retains the same seven g values, points to `data/s1/tfim_open_N<N>_g<g>.json` with exact `E0_exact` and `E1_exact`, and specifies **“Method | VQE only (QPE is not part of this problem).”** Table 4 identifies the N=6 instances as the optional tier. P16 adds the capacity rule: Processor A cannot execute the six-logical-qubit tier; report `not executable: needs N qubits, has M` and explain. The DOCX does not provide a separate N=6-only scoring checklist. The defensible completion standard is therefore to apply the PS-S1 tasks in §8 (locked g grid, seeds 0–2, VQE/HVA, exact references, F/F₂/observables, layer study, and the applicable noisy/resource tasks), while respecting the explicit A-capacity exception. The exact extent to which all §8 noisy/resource subtasks are expected again at N=6 is not separately spelled out; this must not be represented as completed here.

**Completed, supplemental only:** all seven N=6 E0/E1 references are compared with official metadata (tolerance `1e-9`); 204 unique seed starts cover all seven g values (L=1..8 for g=.25, 1.25, 1.5, 2.0; L=1..12 for g=.5, .75, 1.0) × seeds 0–2; 88 optimizer non-success flags and their messages remain preserved in the raw CSV. **Remaining before claiming a full bonus:** no N=6 noisy-D density-matrix study is included, and several minimal-layer selections rely on non-converged optimizer flags (`success=False`).
